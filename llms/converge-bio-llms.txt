# Converge Bio

> Converge Bio is a generative-AI platform for life sciences that accelerates drug development across discovery, molecular design, and manufacturing. It trains Bio-LLM foundation models directly on biological languages (DNA, RNA, and protein sequences) and delivers them through the Converge Computational Lab. There is no public developer API, SDK, or self-serve documentation surface today — the platform is delivered to biotech and pharmaceutical customers under a "Request Access" engagement model.

## Products
- [ConvergeAB (Antibody Design)](https://converge-bio.com/): AI-driven antibody discovery, screening, and optimization across IgG, VHH, and bispecific formats.
- [ConvergeGEO (Gene Expression Optimization)](https://converge-bio.com/): Generative AI for protein-expression optimization across CHO, HEK293, E. coli, K. phaffii, and S. cerevisiae cell systems.
- [ConvergeCELL (Virtual Cell Simulation)](https://converge-bio.com/): Simulates cellular responses across patient populations for target discovery and biomarker identification.
- [Patent Extractor](https://converge-bio.com/): Mines antibody sequences from patents.
